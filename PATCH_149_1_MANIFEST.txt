# PATCH 149.1 MANIFEST — Unit Preview Proof-of-Concept Visibility Hotfix

## Purpose

Patch 149.1 corrects the Patch 149 Unit Preview proof-of-concept layout. The AI audit-loop and DAO/Lido governance proof-of-concept mirrors now appear directly on the first page instead of being hidden behind collapsed dropdown expanders. The DAO/Lido side is also elaborated with compact bullet groups for strengths, risk signals, and a Grok-comparison lens.

## Changed files

- `ui/unit_preview.py`
  - Removed collapsed proof-of-concept expander behavior for the AI audit-loop and DAO/Lido proof-of-concept renderers.
  - Keeps the side-by-side first-page layout.
  - Adds always-visible DAO/Lido detail: strengths/useful design signals, risk/review-pressure signals, and Grok-comparison lens for all four baseline cases.
  - Adds a fallback caption when packaged AI audit-loop screenshots are not present in a checkout.

- `docs/for-reviewers/dao_governance_proof_of_concept.md`

- `tests/test_patch_149_unit_preview_dao_proof_of_concept.py`
