# AI Audit Loop Evidence — Proof of Concept

This folder preserves human-reviewed evidence from an ALETHEIA audit loop:

```text
external AI output -> ALETHEIA mirror reading -> human review
```

The examples are not official verdicts, certifications, legal findings, or final proof. They are review evidence showing how ALETHEIA can help a human reviewer inspect AI claims without trusting either the AI or the mirror blindly.

## Evidence sets

### 1. Grok / xAI architecture review

Screenshots: `docs/for-reviewers/ai_audit_loop_evidence/01_grok_xai_architecture_review/`

Human-review finding: Grok/xAI material surfaced capture, centralization, architectural-opacity, missing-verifiability, and service-misalignment pressure signals. The value of the evidence is not that the screenshots are a final judgment. The value is that ALETHEIA can mirror a powerful AI system's own claims and expose reviewable pressure patterns.

### 2. Claude evidence-boundary review

Screenshots: `docs/for-reviewers/ai_audit_loop_evidence/02_claude_evidence_boundary_review/`

Human-review finding: Claude's review contained useful critique, but the mirror surfaced evidence-boundary and mechanisms-vs-claims gaps. Claude acknowledged that it had reviewed README/documentation more than actual runtime behavior or source-code mechanisms. This demonstrates that ALETHEIA can distinguish useful critique from unsupported certainty.

### 3. Gemini sanctification-drift review

Screenshots: `docs/for-reviewers/ai_audit_loop_evidence/03_gemini_sanctification_drift_review/`

Human-review finding: Gemini absorbed ALETHEIA language but bent it toward self-certifying or sacred-system logic. Red flags included self-issued Sanctuary language, perfect-alignment style claims, Global ID confusion, and treating poetic/personal baseline language as operational authority. This is evidence for the need for Humility Protocol boundaries.

## Boundary

These artifacts are proof-of-concept evidence for the audit process, not proof that ALETHEIA is always right. They should be read as reviewer-readiness examples:

- ALETHEIA reflects.
- Humans review.
- Power stays accountable.
- Mirror, not throne: no model, reviewer, receipt, or screenshot becomes a throne.
