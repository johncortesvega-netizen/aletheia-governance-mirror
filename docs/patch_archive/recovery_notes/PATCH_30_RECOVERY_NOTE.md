# PATCH 30 — Education Defense (EDD)

Type: lightweight diagnostic/scoring guard.

## Purpose

Patch 30 adds Education Defense diagnostics inside the Cognitive Resilience layer. It detects system patterns that thin or replace deep learning, including entertainment compliance, algorithmic isolation, licensed speech, obedience-profile feeds, archive rewriting, and the replacement of books/craft/mentorship/reflection with managed attention loops.

This patch protects the Z-axis as a system property: depth, conscience, craft, intergenerational learning, source checking, and voluntary local practice. It does not judge whether people are intelligent or unintelligent.

## Added signals

- `education_defense_signal`
- `entertainment_compliance_signal`
- `algorithmic_erosion_signal`
- `z_axis_depth_risk_signal`

## Scoring behavior

The adjustment is intentionally bounded and lightweight.

- Protected Education Defense can lightly stabilize local/open/revocable systems.
- Entertainment compliance or algorithmic erosion increases friction, trust friction, and collapse probability.
- High education never launders capture.
- Central power, no appeal, single keyholder, forced delegation, contextual capture, or hard capture still block stabilization.

## Boundaries preserved

Patch 30 does not add:

- global ID sync
- public ledger
- push-warning authority layer
- automatic enforcement
- centralized truth authority
- user/person classification as malicious, smart, or dumb

ALETHEIA remains local-first, witness-based, reviewable, non-sovereign, and mirror-not-throne.

Power → Mirror. Never Mirror → Power.
