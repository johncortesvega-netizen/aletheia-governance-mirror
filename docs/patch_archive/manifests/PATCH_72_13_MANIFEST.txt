Purpose:
- Fix the Evidence Lab Country-Year Explorer year dropdown snapping back to the synced/default year such as 2024 after the user chooses another available year.
- Clarify direct merged-upload diagnostics so `empirical_trust_prior` is presented as a derived/scoring field, not a missing upload source.
- Keep trust raw survey diagnostics active when `wvs_generalized_trust` is present.

- The unscored merged evidence-table message explains that raw trust is read from `wvs_generalized_trust` and trust prior is derived during scoring.
