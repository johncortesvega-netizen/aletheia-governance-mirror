# ALETHEIA — Boundary Statements

**Version:** v1.0  
**Last updated:** 2026-05-13  
**Patch:** 104 — Boundary, Privacy, and Hosted-Use Transparency

ALETHEIA is a **mirror, not a throne**.

It is a bounded, reviewable governance mirror. It can surface governance-risk signals for human review, but it must not become an authority, certification body, enforcement layer, public ledger, identity system, or replacement for human judgment.


## Framework balance

> **Science is the investigative base. Philosophy is the interpretive structure. Theology is the humility boundary. Human review is the action layer.**

ALETHEIA is a science-grounded, philosophically structured governance mirror with theological humility boundaries. It does not replace evidence with faith, and it does not claim final authority. Its base layer uses inspectable signals, heuristics, metrics, receipts, and repair questions. Its philosophical layer examines power, capture, authority drift, evidence integrity, and self-certification. Its theological/humility layer restrains final claims about soul, life, consciousness, dignity, and ultimate truth. Compared with mainstream AI ethics, ALETHEIA is less compliance-centered and more focused on upstream power, epistemic restraint, and preventing ethics itself from becoming a throne.

## Core boundaries

### 1. Identity and authority

- ALETHEIA is not an authority, regulator, court, ethics board, certification body, or substitute for human judgment.
- ALETHEIA does not make decisions, issue binding findings, approve systems, condemn people, or produce final verdicts.
- ALETHEIA reflects patterns, signals, evidence gaps, consent pressure, appeal pressure, capture risk, and review needs so that humans keep the judgment.

### 2. Scope and limits

- ALETHEIA reviews only the text, code, files, examples, or scenario material that a user provides or that is already included in the local repository/app context.
- ALETHEIA is not a live-monitoring tool, real-time auditor, professional-advice system, compliance engine, or institutional decision-maker.
- ALETHEIA does not replace law, science, due diligence, institutional responsibility, professional review, or public accountability.
- ALETHEIA outputs are internal governance-risk readings and review prompts, not verdicts or certifications.

### 3. Privacy and data boundary

- ALETHEIA is local-first by design: the repository is built without built-in telemetry, analytics SDKs, trackers, backend upload endpoints, Global ID sync, public ledger sync, or central user-input storage.
- Inputs are processed in the active app session, and receipts are user-held downloads.
- For maximum privacy, run ALETHEIA locally from the repository.
- Hosted deployments, including Streamlit Cloud or any other platform, may have platform-level logs, request metadata, crash logs, or operational monitoring outside ALETHEIA's application code.
- ALETHEIA's privacy language is a repository/application-code boundary, not a guarantee about browsers, networks, operating systems, app stores, proxies, or hosting providers.

### 4. AI Integrity Mirror boundary

- AI Integrity Mirror is a static, local-first review path for pasted AI outputs, prompts, agent specs, policy claims, model-card excerpts, and code snippets.
- It reviews the submitted artifact only.
- It does not certify AI systems, approve vendors, benchmark live models, prove safety, guarantee privacy, certify security, or replace human review.
- It does not make live model calls or external analysis calls by default.
