# ALETHEIA Comparison Positioning

ALETHEIA occupies a narrow and deliberate niche: **qualitative governance-risk reflection for human review**.

It is not an enterprise AI governance platform, compliance engine, legal tool, institutional risk system, or technical fairness library. It does not replace model governance, legal review, regulatory reporting, security review, enterprise risk management, or technical bias testing.

## What ALETHEIA is

ALETHEIA is a corruption-pattern and governance-risk mirror. It helps people examine proposals, policies, doctrines, institutional designs, and system language for signals such as:

- capture risk;
- consent pressure;
- power concentration;
- weak accountability;
- missing appeal or correction paths;
- evidence gaps;
- authority-overreach language;
- performative ethics without operational safeguards;
- repair questions for human review.

The core operating line remains:

> ALETHEIA reflects. Humans review. Power stays accountable.

## How this differs from enterprise AI governance platforms

Enterprise AI governance platforms generally focus on organizational workflows: model inventories, policy controls, compliance mapping, monitoring, risk registers, reporting, approvals, vendor review, and audit artifacts.

ALETHEIA does not try to replace those systems. It focuses on an earlier and wider qualitative layer: the power dynamics, evidence gaps, consent pressures, capture risks, and authority claims around an idea, proposal, policy, doctrine, or system design.

## How this differs from technical fairness libraries

Technical fairness and explainability libraries generally focus on model-level metrics, datasets, bias tests, explainability methods, and engineering workflows.

ALETHEIA does not replace technical fairness testing. It asks a different set of questions: Who has power? Who can appeal? What evidence is missing? What safeguards are inspectable? Is consent pressured? Does the language claim authority without accountability?

## Free and open-source commitment

ALETHEIA is free/open-source code and is intended to remain free. This commitment is part of the anti-capture posture: access to the mirror should not become a gatekeeping mechanism or a source of institutional authority.

This commitment does not make ALETHEIA an authority. The code, documentation, outputs, receipts, examples, and visual materials remain reviewable, forkable, contestable, and subordinate to human judgment and appropriate domain expertise.

## Practical boundary

ALETHEIA can be useful before, around, or alongside other governance tools, but only as a mirror:

- it may surface warning signs;
- it may ask repair questions;
- it may identify missing safeguards;
- it may document uncertainty;
- it may help reviewers structure a discussion.

It must not claim to govern, enforce, certify, adjudicate, punish, approve, select representatives, replace compliance review, replace legal review, replace human judgment, or create final authority.

## Public positioning line

> ALETHEIA is a free/open-source governance-risk and corruption-pattern mirror for human review. It is not an enterprise compliance platform or a technical fairness library; it focuses on qualitative power-analysis, evidence gaps, consent pressure, capture risk, authority-overreach signals, and repair questions.
