# ALETHEIA Beginner UX — Try This First

Patch 118 polishes the beginner-facing guide introduced in Patch 111. The goal is to make the first-use path clearer and more actionable without hiding the review boundary or turning ALETHEIA into an authority.

## First safe path

1. Open **Mirror Check**.
2. Paste a short governance, AI-policy, institutional, or product claim.
3. Run the reading.
4. Read the **risk reading** and observed reasons.
5. Review the repair questions before relying on the result.
6. Download the receipt if you want a local record.

## First-audit checklist

Use the checklist before relying on a reading:

- Is the input short enough that you can inspect the result yourself?
- Does the reading show which pressure signals were observed?
- Are there repair questions you can answer with evidence?
- Is there any claim about people, law, health, safety, legitimacy, or institutional action that requires outside review?
- Are you using the local version if the material is sensitive?

## What the reading means

ALETHEIA can help a reviewer notice pressure around power, consent, evidence, appeal, capture risk, and human review. A stronger pressure reading means the input deserves more review. It does not prove wrongdoing, truth, legitimacy, safety, or compliance.

## What the reading does not mean

The reading is not a verdict, certification, approval, legal finding, safety guarantee, privacy guarantee, compliance approval, or final-truth claim. It should not be used to punish, rank, blacklist, approve, or reject people, organizations, AI systems, or institutions.

## Stop and review if

- the result would affect a person's rights, access, reputation, or safety;
- the source evidence is missing, stale, unclear, or one-sided;
- the text involves legal, medical, political, institutional, or financial consequences;
- you cannot explain the receipt in plain language to another reviewer.

## What the guide is

The guide is static UI copy rendered from `ui/beginner_guide.py`. It helps a new user find a safe first path through the app and adds a simple first-audit checklist.

## What the guide is not

The guide is not a scoring change, verdict-routing change, signal-pattern change, signal-weight change, receipt schema change, module-routing change, certification, approval path, compliance workflow, enforcement mechanism, privacy guarantee, or final-truth claim.

## Boundary language

The guide must keep the following boundary visible:

- ALETHEIA surfaces signals.
- Humans keep the judgment.
- Readings are not verdicts or certifications.
- Sensitive audits should be run locally.
- Hosted deployments may have platform-level logs outside ALETHEIA's application-code boundary.
