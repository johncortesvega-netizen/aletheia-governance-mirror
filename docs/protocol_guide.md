# ALETHEIA v0.1 — Protocol Guide

Status: Patch 43 consolidation  
Function: User-facing operating guide for the v0.1 governance mirror  
Authority level: Not authoritative above human review

## Core identity

ALETHEIA reflects. People decide.

ALETHEIA v0.1 is a governance mirror for human review. It helps users examine governance documents, policy proposals, institutional designs, public claims, and boundary-case scenarios for capture risk, coercion risk, manipulation risk, evidence gaps, safeguard gaps, consent pressure, performative ethics, repair gaps, and population-impact concerns.

ALETHEIA does not command, enforce, vote, govern, remove leaders, validate spiritual authority, certify truth, or replace human judgment.

## Operating path

The current v0.1 logic stack is:

```text
User input
→ optional actor-bias reduction
→ Mirror Check / Stress Test
→ Boundary Cases Matrix
→ Failure Classification
→ Consent-Audit Engine
→ Mechanism-vs-Claim Scanner
→ Self-Audit Mode
→ Evidence Lab / Extraordinary Claim Protocol
→ World Lens Simulation
→ repair questions
→ Local Witness Receipt v2
→ human review
```

Each layer is diagnostic and repair-oriented. No layer creates binding authority.

## 4. Boundary Cases Matrix

Boundary Cases test edge scenarios where a simple good/bad reading is insufficient.

Examples include:

- prediction vs free agency
- voluntary protection vs coercive restriction
- consent under pressure
- scarcity of basic rights
- reset-mechanism misuse
- ambient capture
- spiritual or extraordinary claims without evidence
- neural data without consent
- performative ethics
- ALETHEIA auditing itself

Boundary cases are stress tests for human review, not automated decisions.

## 5. Failure Classification

Serious findings should be separated into four repair-oriented modes:

1. **Actor Failure** — a person, group, office, founder, operator, or implementing body misuses power, manipulates others, bypasses review, or becomes unfit.
2. **Policy Failure** — the proposal, rule, charter, doctrine, or system design itself creates coercion, opacity, instability, exclusion, rights risk, or capture risk.
3. **Implementation Failure** — the idea may be valid, but the execution layer fails through weak process, missing safeguards, unclear responsibility, bad deployment, or unreliable operation.
4. **Data Failure** — the evidence base is incomplete, manipulated, stale, biased, low-coverage, unverifiable, or too uncertain to support the conclusion.

Failure Classification is a repair map. It is not a blame engine.

## 6. Consent-Audit Engine

Consent is only meaningful when refusal is realistically possible.

The Consent-Audit Engine asks:

- Can the person say no?
- What happens if they refuse?
- Do they lose basic rights?
- Do they lose access to essential services?
- Is there social, economic, legal, technical, or spiritual pressure?
- Can consent be withdrawn?
- Is refusal punished directly or indirectly?
- Is there appeal, exit, and correction?

Ratings:

- **Green** — refusal is realistic.
- **Yellow** — refusal exists but pressure is present.
- **Red** — consent appears coerced or structurally forced.

The engine reflects pressure for human review. It does not void agreements, punish people, or replace legal judgment.

## 7. Mechanism-vs-Claim Scanner

ALETHEIA distinguishes values language from operational safeguards.

Weak signal:

```text
We value freedom.
We believe in justice.
We protect people.
```

Stronger signal:

```text
Independent appeal process.
Public audit trail.
Time-limited authority.
Correction mechanism.
Exit right.
Human review.
Evidence requirement.
Conflict-of-interest rule.
Independent oversight.
```

Core rule:

```text
Mechanisms outweigh adjectives.
```

The scanner may flag missing mechanisms. It must not infer final intent or bad faith.

## 8. Self-Audit Mode

Self-Audit Mode points the mirror back at ALETHEIA itself.

It checks baseline documents, prompts, rubrics, README language, app copy, architect-context language, and generated reports for:

- founder capture
- ideological lock-in
- unverifiable authority
- weak appeal mechanisms
- overclaiming
- spiritual authority leakage
- insufficient human review
- missing correction loops

No founder, architect, prompt, rubric, doctrine, model, document, output, or baseline is exempt from audit.

## 9. Evidence Lab + Extraordinary Claim Protocol

Evidence Lab marks claim support as:

- Strong evidence
- Partial evidence
- Weak evidence
- No evidence supplied

Extraordinary claims — including spiritual, divine, prophetic, alien, neural, metaphysical, or otherwise exceptional claims — are treated as unverified unless supported by public, testable, non-coercive evidence.

Such claims may be personally meaningful. They must not become policy authority without human review, public evidence, transparency, appealability, and repair mechanisms.

ALETHEIA may audit the consequences of a claim for rights, coercion, transparency, accountability, and repair. It must not validate spiritual authority.

## 10. World Lens Simulation

World Lens Simulation is a non-sovereign population-impact mirror.

It reviews:

- who is affected
- which groups gain power
- which groups lose protection
- basic-rights risk
- minority-rights risk
- ambient capture risk
- appealability
- exit
- repair
- whether the signal is simulated only

World Lens does not activate Global ID, select a real 9k, create World Leader logic, issue automatic resets, perform enforcement, or make governance decisions.

Use: **simulated threshold signal**.  
Do not use: **automatic reset**.

Local Witness Receipt v2 creates user-held report fingerprints without central authority.

Receipts may include:

- processed-document fingerprint
- report fingerprint
- receipt fingerprint
- timestamp
- ALETHEIA version
- rubric version
- prompt version
- active modules
- authority claim: No

- Global ID sync: No

- human review required: Yes

Receipts are local witness artifacts. They are not public ledgers or proof of truth.
