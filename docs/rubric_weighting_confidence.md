# Rubric Weighting and Confidence Notes

Status: v0.2 candidate / v1.0 documentation boundary
Purpose: Make scoring more transparent without making scores authoritative

## Principle

A score is a review signal, not a verdict.

Rubric weighting and confidence notes should help reviewers understand uncertainty, evidence gaps, and the basis for each signal.

## Possible Future Fields

For each factor, future reports may show:

- assigned score
- confidence level
- evidence from text
- missing evidence
- why the score was assigned
- what would lower the score
- what would raise the score
- reviewer note

## Confidence Levels

Suggested labels:

- High confidence: clear text evidence and clear mechanism
- Medium confidence: partial evidence or ambiguous mechanism
- Low confidence: weak evidence, missing details, or high uncertainty
- Insufficient evidence: no reliable basis for a score

## Weighting Rule

Any weighting system must remain visible, adjustable for review, documented, and non-authoritative.

Factor weighting must not hide coercion, basic-rights risk, founder capture, missing appeal, missing correction, or evidence gaps.

## Safety Boundary

Confidence notes may reduce overclaiming. They must not make ALETHEIA a final authority.
