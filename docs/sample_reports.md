# ALETHEIA v0.1 — Sample Reports

Patch 46 adds example reports so users can understand ALETHEIA output before uploading their own documents.

These examples are demonstration artifacts only. They are not legal advice, policy commands, governance decisions, religious validation, or final judgments.

## Included Examples

- `examples/example_policy_audit.md` — sample Mirror Check policy audit.
- `examples/example_boundary_case.md` — sample Boundary Case report for consent under pressure.
- `examples/example_self_audit.md` — sample Self-Audit report for Aletheia baseline language.
- `examples/example_witness_receipt.md` — sample Local Witness Receipt v2.

## How to Read the Examples

Use the examples to see the expected structure:

1. Summary
2. Risk signal
3. Evidence or text basis
4. Failure classification
5. Recommended safeguards
6. Human review disclaimer

## Safe Interpretation

A sample report shows how ALETHEIA reflects risk. It does not prove that a policy, person, institution, doctrine, or system is good or bad.

ALETHEIA reflects. Humans review. Power stays accountable.
