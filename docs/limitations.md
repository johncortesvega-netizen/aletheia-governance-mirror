# ALETHEIA v0.1 — Limitations

ALETHEIA v0.1 is a governance-risk research prototype. It is a mirror for human review, not a decision authority.

## Non-authority boundary

ALETHEIA must not be treated as:

- legal advice;
- political authority;
- medical, psychological, or religious authority;
- a sovereign system;
- an election mechanism;
- a public safety command system;
- an automated enforcement tool;
- a way to remove leaders or govern people.

The correct interpretation is:

> This output identifies a governance-risk pattern worth reviewing.

Not:

> The AI has decided.

## Evidence limitations

ALETHEIA can only review the input it receives and the evidence attached to it. Missing, stale, biased, incomplete, or low-coverage evidence can produce incomplete outputs.

Evidence labels such as Strong, Partial, Weak, or No evidence supplied are review signals, not final truth verdicts.

## Scoring limitations

Capture Risk, Sanctuary alignment, consent integrity, world-lens risk, and mechanism coverage are structured audit signals. They are not objective proof that a system is good, bad, legal, illegal, legitimate, illegitimate, divine, corrupt, or safe.

Scores should always be read with:

- the cited text;
- evidence gaps;
- uncertainty notes;
- repair questions;
- human review.

## Extraordinary claim limitations

Spiritual, divine, prophetic, alien, neural, metaphysical, or otherwise exceptional claims are treated as unverified unless supported by public, testable, non-coercive evidence.

Such claims may be personally meaningful. They must not be used by ALETHEIA to validate authority, remove guardrails, bypass appeal, or replace human review.

## Archive caution

Some historical archive material may contain AI-flattery artifacts or inflated validation language. These materials may be preserved as development history, but they are not independent proof, founder validation, governance justification, or evidence that ALETHEIA is correct.

## Prototype caution

v0.1 intentionally avoids:

- real Global ID sync;
- real 9k selection;
- real World Leader logic;
- automatic resets;
- public ledger authority;
- neural data;
- memory extraction;
- central citizen storage;
- automated enforcement.

## Final limitation

ALETHEIA reflects. Humans review. Power stays accountable.
