# Validation and Precision Framing

ALETHEIA uses internal rule-based and heuristic readings to support human review. Numerical values are review aids. They are not independent validation, scientific certainty, compliance approval, certification, or final truth.

## What the numbers mean

ALETHEIA numbers summarize internal readings such as integrity, friction, trust index, alignment, ego pressure, collapse pressure, coverage, and threshold mapping. They help reviewers compare outputs inside the project.

They do not prove that a proposal, institution, country, policy, AI artifact, or system is safe, lawful, ethical, legitimate, or trustworthy.

## Decimal precision boundary

A value such as `0.7311` or `Z=0.9999` should be read as an internal display value, not as scientific certainty. Decimal precision exists so repeated runs and receipt comparisons can be inspected consistently. It does not mean the underlying governance question has been independently measured to that precision.

## Z-axis and asymptote boundary

The Z-axis maps proximity to the human/system authority boundary. It is not a perfection score. `Z=1.0000` is outside ALETHEIA's claim: beyond code, metrics, receipts, hashes, trees, 9k structures, and institutional power.

## World Lens boundary

World Lens selected-year evidence views are empirical evidence bundles and internal taxonomy readings. They are not country certifications, government ratings, political mandates, or final verdicts.

World Lens distinguishes raw trust survey evidence from trust-prior/fallback coverage. Trust-prior coverage must not be presented as observed public trust.

## Internal consistency is not independent validation

Internal correlation checks, baseline checks, fixture tests, and receipt comparisons are useful for reviewability. They do not become independent external validation when ALETHEIA's own fields or assumptions are part of the target being checked.

## External validation roadmap

Future independent validation work should remain separate from internal consistency checks. Useful directions include:

- compare selected readings against independent conflict, coup, democratic backsliding, institutional-failure, or public-safety datasets;
- document false positives and false negatives;
- add benchmark-style fixtures for known governance-risk patterns;
- separate internal consistency checks from independent validation;
- invite external reviewers to challenge rubric assumptions;
- publish disagreements and failures as review material, not as embarrassment to hide.

## Correct public language

Use:

- internal governance-risk reading;
- review aid;
- heuristic/rule-based signal;
- selected-year evidence view;
- human review required;
- local witness receipt.

Avoid:

- certified;
- proven safe;
- legally valid;
- official rating;
- final verdict;
- objective truth;
- automated approval;
- enforcement decision.

ALETHEIA reflects. Humans review. Power stays accountable.
