# ALETHEIA Glossary for Reviewers

This glossary explains project-specific language in plain terms. Each term is a reviewer aid, not an authority claim.

## Mirror, not throne

**Plain meaning:** ALETHEIA reflects patterns for human review; it does not rule.

**What it does:** Keeps outputs framed as readings, signals, repair prompts, and evidence context.

**What it does not claim:** No certification, approval, rejection, enforcement, legal authority, political authority, medical authority, religious authority, or final truth.

**Where it appears:** README, app header/footer, receipts, Protocol Guide, Why ALETHEIA, boundary docs.

## Human review required

**Plain meaning:** A person remains responsible for interpretation and action.

**What it does:** Anchors every module and receipt to external human judgment.

**What it does not claim:** ALETHEIA cannot approve, reject, enforce, or release decisions by itself.

**Where it appears:** Receipts, boundary notices, public docs, Protocol Guide.

## Aletheia Unit Preview

**Plain meaning:** The first-page beginner path that helps users understand where to start.

**What it does:** Routes examples toward likely modules and keeps the full app behind a calmer entry point.

**What it does not claim:** It does not score, certify, or decide the user's case.

**Where it appears:** App start page / Unit Preview.

## Local witness receipt

**Plain meaning:** A user-held receipt recording a local ALETHEIA reading.

**What it does:** Preserves native values, hashes, authority-boundary statements, and repair questions for review.

**What it does not claim:** It is not an enforcement action, public ledger entry, legal record, certification, or proof of safety.

**Where it appears:** Downloaded receipts and Receipt Reader.

## Receipt Reader

**Plain meaning:** Upload-only support utility for reading ALETHEIA receipts in Standard View.

**What it does:** Explains uploaded receipt values verbally and shows native values.

**What it does not claim:** It does not rescore, override, regenerate, certify, or merge verdicts.

**Where it appears:** Support utility below the main modules.

## Standard View

**Plain meaning:** A readable translation of an uploaded receipt.

**What it does:** Turns native receipt fields into a human-readable brief and observation cards.

**What it does not claim:** It is not a new score, new verdict, or external standard.

**Where it appears:** Receipt Reader.

## 9k

**Plain meaning:** A human anti-tyranny scaffold / threshold steward concept used in World Lens allocation language.

**What it does:** Helps describe selected-year evidence allocation and representation scaffolding inside the project.

**What it does not claim:** It is not a real governing body, divine authority, public ledger, voting system, Global ID system, or final legitimacy source.

**Where it appears:** World Lens, receipts, threshold steward notes, selected-year allocation docs.

## Sydney Protocol

**Plain meaning:** The project's boundary and humility protocol for keeping ALETHEIA from becoming an authority.

**What it does:** Frames repair, appealability, anti-capture, non-divinization, human review, and mirror-not-throne constraints.

**What it does not claim:** It is not law, religion, public policy, institutional authority, or final truth.

**Where it appears:** Protocol Guide, receipts, Why ALETHEIA, humility/asymptote notes.

## V-Axis

**Plain meaning:** A review axis used to think about movement toward or away from review boundaries and capture pressure.

**What it does:** Supports scenario/simulation language and internal review framing.

**What it does not claim:** It is not an objective law of governance or independently validated scientific scale.

**Where it appears:** Stress Test and older conceptual docs.

## World Lens

**Plain meaning:** Selected-year country evidence view using empirical datasets and population/seat allocation context.

**What it does:** Shows internal taxonomy distribution, coverage, integrity, friction, collapse pressure, and evidence-table context.

**What it does not claim:** It is not a country certification, government rating, election mechanism, political mandate, or final truth.

**Where it appears:** World Lens module and World Lens evidence bundle receipts.

## Evidence Lab

**Plain meaning:** Evidence/context review support surface.

**What it does:** Helps organize source posture and evidence context for review.

**What it does not claim:** It does not prove final truth or replace expert investigation.

**Where it appears:** Evidence Lab module.

## AI Integrity Mirror

**Plain meaning:** Static artifact reviewer for pasted AI-related text/code snippets.

**What it does:** Surfaces overclaim, opacity, missing human review, manipulation, privacy-boundary pressure, unsafe code patterns, and authority drift in uploaded/pasted artifacts.

**What it does not claim:** It does not test live models, vendors, deployments, full repositories, hidden system prompts, training data, or future behavior. It is not AI certification.

**Where it appears:** AI Integrity Mirror module and AI Integrity receipts.

## Stress Test

**Plain meaning:** Scenario review surface for governance pressure cases.

**What it does:** Reviews scenarios for safeguard pressure, appealability, capture risk, friction, collapse pressure, and repair questions.

**What it does not claim:** It does not predict real-world outcomes, enforce action, or create binding policy.

**Where it appears:** Stress Test module and batch receipts.

## Boundary Cases

**Plain meaning:** Reference/calibration layer for hard cases.

**What it does:** Helps compare edge-case patterns against ALETHEIA's boundaries.

**What it does not claim:** It does not create receipts, decide cases, or overrule module readings.

**Where it appears:** Boundary Cases module after World Lens.

## SANCTUARY

**Plain meaning:** Low-pressure internal reading.

**What it does:** Indicates the uploaded scenario/artifact/receipt has low internal review pressure under ALETHEIA's taxonomy.

**What it does not claim:** It is not final safety, final truth, legal approval, or certification.

**Where it appears:** Receipts, Mirror Check, Stress Test, AI Integrity, World Lens internal taxonomy.

## THRESHOLD

**Plain meaning:** Review-needed internal reading.

**What it does:** Indicates unresolved safeguard, appealability, transparency, evidence, or friction pressure.

**What it does not claim:** It is not a command to accept or reject anything.

**Where it appears:** Receipts and module outputs.

## ASYLUM

**Plain meaning:** High-pressure internal reading requiring escalation-level human review.

**What it does:** Flags strong capture/collapse/coercion/authority-boundary pressure.

**What it does not claim:** It does not enforce action, punish, ban, or become emergency authority.

**Where it appears:** Receipts and module outputs.

## QUESTION_PROMPT

**Plain meaning:** Review-tool mode for questions/audit prompts, not a scored risk state.

**What it does:** Preserves audit questions and repair prompts without scoring them as scenarios.

**What it does not claim:** It is not a fourth risk state and does not carry scored metrics.

**Where it appears:** Mirror Check and batch question receipts.

## Z-axis / Asymptote note

**Plain meaning:** A humility boundary showing proximity to what human/system tools may responsibly claim.

**What it does:** Reminds reviewers that maximum system-readable values remain below final authority.

**What it does not claim:** Z=1.0000 is outside ALETHEIA's claim; human/system scenarios must not be treated as final truth.

**Where it appears:** Threshold mapping layer and receipts.

## Public Trust Package

**Plain meaning:** A map of documents reviewers can inspect.

**What it does:** Points to boundary, privacy, hosting limits, signal transparency, patch history, and public review checklist.

**What it does not claim:** It is not certification, security proof, or tamper-proof control.

**Where it appears:** `docs/public_trust_package.md` and Protocol Guide.
