# Manual Evidence Attachment

Status: v0.2 candidate / v1.0 documentation boundary
Purpose: Let users attach evidence without pretending ALETHEIA independently verified it

## Principle

User-supplied evidence can improve review, but it must remain clearly labeled as user-supplied unless independently checked.

## Evidence Attachment Fields

Future versions may let users provide:

- source title
- source URL or citation text
- source type
- date accessed
- claim supported
- claim contradicted
- reviewer notes
- confidence level

## Required Labels

Attached evidence should be labeled as:

- user-supplied
- not independently verified by ALETHEIA
- subject to human review
- incomplete unless source coverage is sufficient

## Extraordinary Claims

For spiritual, divine, prophetic, alien, neural, metaphysical, or otherwise exceptional claims:

- personal meaning may be acknowledged
- policy authority is not granted
- public, testable, non-coercive evidence is required for governance use
- human review remains required

## Safety Boundary

Manual evidence attachment must not become automatic truth certification.
