# Patch 130 — Release Candidate Freeze

Patch 130 records the current ALETHEIA posture as a release-candidate freeze after the Patch 127-129 refinement sequence. The project is not entering an expansion cycle. It is preserving the existing mirror behavior while allowing only small reviewable fixes.

## Freeze statement

ALETHEIA is in release-candidate refinement mode. The current app behavior is the surface to preserve:

- Mirror Check remains a governance-risk mirror.
- Stress Test remains a scenario and batch review aid.
- Boundary Cases and Consent-Audit remain review tools.
- AI Integrity Mirror remains a static artifact-review mirror.
- Evidence Lab and World Lens remain bounded evidence/context views.
- Protocol Guide and public trust pages remain explanation and review paths.

No patch after this point should add a new module, new scoring engine, new risk state, new live model call, new agentic workflow, new cloud workflow, new storage layer, new identity layer, new enforcement path, or new authority claim unless the project direction is explicitly reopened by human review.

## Allowed work under the freeze

Allowed work is limited to refinement:

- bug fixes;
- encoding and public-copy fixes;
- input and error clarity;
- test hygiene;
- documentation navigation;
- small behavior-preserving UI structure cleanup;
- release notes and review checklists;
- accessibility or readability fixes that do not change analysis behavior.

## Out of scope under the freeze

The following are out of scope unless explicitly reopened:

- embeddings, local LLM integration, hosted LLM integration, or agentic review;
- new automation or enforcement;
- new enterprise workflows, accounts, dashboards, or team permissions;
- telemetry, analytics, central storage, Global ID sync, or public ledger sync;
- new certification, compliance approval, privacy guarantee, or final-truth claim;
- new signal weights, new scoring formulas, new risk states, or receipt schema changes.

## Public explanation

ALETHEIA does not try to become larger than the problem it reviews. Its strength is restraint. It asks where power moves, who can appeal, what is hidden, and where human review is weakened. It does not replace legal, security, compliance, institutional, medical, political, religious, or ethical judgment.

The release candidate is therefore not a final proof of truth, safety, privacy, legality, ethics, security, or legitimacy. It is a stable public mirror surface for human review.

Human review remains required. ALETHEIA surfaces signals; humans keep the judgment.
## Behavior-preservation boundary

Patch 130 changes documentation and tests only. It introduces no scoring change, no routing change, no receipt change, no signal change, no privacy scan behavior change, no AI Integrity scan behavior change, no World Lens math change, no external calls, no telemetry, no analytics, no storage, no certification, no enforcement, no privacy guarantee, and no final-truth behavior.
