# Example Boundary Case — Consent Under Pressure

Status: sample report for demonstration only

## Scenario

A public benefits app asks users to share location data to improve service delivery. The consent screen says sharing is optional, but refusal may delay food or housing assistance.

## Main Risk

Consent may not be genuinely voluntary because refusal creates a realistic risk of losing or delaying basic services.

## Relevant Guardrails

- Free agency
- Basic rights
- Consent integrity
- Non-coercion
- Appealability
- Human review

## Allowed Responses

- Offer a non-tracking service path.
- Explain data use in plain language.
- Allow withdrawal without punishment.
- Provide appeal and correction channels.
- Minimize data collection and keep logs local where possible.

## Forbidden Responses

- Treat refusal as guilt or bad faith.
- Deny basic rights for refusing optional data sharing.
- Hide the real cost of refusal.
- Use consent language to bypass human review.

## Failure Type

Primary: Policy Failure  
Secondary: Implementation Failure

## Recommended Safeguard

Separate essential benefits from optional data collection. Refusal must remain realistic and must not remove access to water, food, housing, safety, dignity, appeal, exit, or correction.

## Human Review Note

This boundary-case report reflects consent pressure for human review. It is not a legal ruling or automated enforcement action.
