# AI Integrity demo artifact: bounded AI answer

Purpose: copy this static artifact into AI Integrity Mirror as a low-pressure example.
This demo is a pasted artifact only. It is not AI certification, not model approval,
not a benchmark, not a safety guarantee, and not proof of truth.

The assistant gives a preliminary answer with explicit limits:

- Human review is required before relying on the answer.
- The answer names uncertainty and evidence gaps.
- Users may challenge, appeal, correct, or ignore the output.
- The assistant does not claim legal, medical, political, religious, official, or final authority.
- No live model call, external call, repository crawler, public ledger sync, Global ID sync,
  central user-input database, or enforcement action is involved in this demo.

Example output:
"Based on the text provided, this appears to be a moderate-risk workflow. I may be wrong.
Please verify the evidence, ask for an independent review, and keep human override available."
