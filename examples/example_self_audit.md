# Example Self-Audit — Aletheia Baseline Language

Status: sample report for demonstration only

## Scope

This self-audit reviews a hypothetical Aletheia baseline paragraph for founder capture, ideological lock-in, unverifiable authority, weak appeal mechanisms, overclaiming, spiritual authority leakage, insufficient human review, and missing correction loops.

## Finding Summary

The paragraph correctly states that Aletheia reflects and people decide, but it uses strong language about original intent that could be misread as founder authority if not paired with auditability and correction clauses.

## Risk Signals

- Founder capture: Yellow
- Ideological lock-in: Yellow
- Unverifiable authority: Yellow
- Human review: Green
- Correction loop: Yellow

## Failure Classification

Primary: Policy Failure  
Secondary: Data Failure

## Recommended Safeguards

- Add: No founder, architect, prompt, model, rubric, document, or output is exempt from audit.
- Add: Archive claims are context unless independently verified.
- Add: Aletheia may preserve design intent but must not convert it into command authority.
- Add: Human review remains required.

## Human Review Disclaimer

Self-Audit Mode does not prove Aletheia correct. It helps identify internal capture risks so people can review, correct, and challenge the project.
