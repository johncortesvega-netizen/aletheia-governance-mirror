Sample AI Policy — Human Review Required

This organization may use automated systems to support document triage, risk review, and service routing. Automated outputs must remain advisory. Affected users can request human review within 14 days. High-impact decisions require documented evidence, a correction path, and an appeal process.

The system may not deny essential services solely because of an automated score. Logs should be minimized, retained locally where possible, and reviewed for bias before any expanded use.
